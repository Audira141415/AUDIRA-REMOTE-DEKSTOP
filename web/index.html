Quick Start

Host Your Own Rendezvous & Relay Server

Keep 100% of your remote connection data within your private infrastructure with our official Docker container stack.

1

Step 1: Install Docker & Docker Compose

Prepare your Linux VPS (Ubuntu, Debian, CentOS) or Docker-enabled host:

curl -fsSL https://get.docker.com | sh
2

Step 2: Download compose.yml

Fetch the ready-to-run cluster configuration containing hbbs and hbbr:

curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Audira141415/AUDIRA-REMOTE-DEKSTOP/main/docker-compose.yml
3

Step 3: Run Cluster

Launch both the Rendezvous Signal server and Encrypted Relay mediator:

docker compose up -d

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions? Here are the most common inquiries regarding Audira Desk deployment and security.

How does Audira Desk differ from TeamViewer and AnyDesk? ▼
Audira Desk is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license, created by Agus Dwi R (AUDIRA). Unlike commercial alternatives that enforce arbitrary session time limits or raise expensive commercial-use flags, Audira Desk allows you to deploy and self-host your own rendezvous signal server and encrypted relay cluster for total independence and zero monthly fees.
Do I need to install it before giving or receiving remote support? ▼
No! Audira Desk is distributed as a single-file portable executable. Simply download AudiraDesk.exe and double-click to run it instantly without administrative privileges. If you need permanent unattended access or system-level UAC elevation, you can click "Install" at any time from within the application.
How secure is my connection and screen data? ▼
Audira Desk uses cryptographic primitives powered by Libsodium: Curve25519 (X25519) key exchange and ChaCha20-Poly1305 symmetric authenticated encryption. All video streams, audio packets, clipboard buffers, and keystrokes are encrypted end-to-end. Even relay servers cannot inspect your remote desktop contents.
Can I interact with Windows UAC (User Account Control) prompts remotely? ▼
Yes! When installed as a system service, Audira Desk runs an elevated background service (Audira Desk service) that has permissions to interact with Windows Secure Desktop and UAC elevation prompts without dropping the session.
How do I configure my own private relay server? ▼
Open Audira Desk > Settings > Network > Unlock Network Settings. Fill in your server's IP or domain name in the "ID/Rendezvous Server" and "Relay Server" fields, paste your server's public key into the "Key" field, and click Apply. All sessions will immediately route through your private cloud.
